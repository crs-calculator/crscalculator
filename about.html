About This Calculator

Last updated: November 17, 2026

The CRS Calculator is a free, independent tool that estimates your Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) score for Canada's Express Entry system. It is built for students, workers and families who want a fast, honest answer to one question: how many CRS points do I have?

What the calculator uses

The scoring rules come directly from the official Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) pages: the CRS criteria page, the Express Entry changes page, the high-wage occupations list, and the language test equivalency charts. The calculator is updated for the November 3, 2026 changes, including the new Labour Market Integration factor and the 1,270 point maximum.

What it does not do

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to IRCC or the Government of Canada. It does not provide legal or immigration advice, and it does not store, assess or submit any profile. Your official score is calculated by IRCC when you submit an Express Entry profile. Always confirm important decisions with the official IRCC CRS tool or a licensed immigration professional.

Privacy by design

Every calculation happens in your browser. Your inputs are never uploaded, stored or shared. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Keeping the grid current

IRCC can change the CRS grid, the high-wage occupation list, or draw procedures at any time. This site is reviewed whenever IRCC publishes a change, and the update date on the calculator page shows which rules are in force. If you spot an error, please use the contact page.
