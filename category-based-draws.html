Category-Based Draws in Express Entry, Explained

Last updated: November 18, 2026. Source: IRCC news backgrounder on 2026 categories, published February 18, 2026, read November 2026.

Since 2023, IRCC has invited Express Entry candidates through category-based rounds alongside general and program-specific rounds. In a category-based draw, IRCC invites candidates who are already in the Express Entry pool and eligible for a specific category, such as a healthcare occupation or French-language proficiency. Candidates are ranked by CRS score within that category. This guide lists the 2026 categories, explains the 1-year experience rule, and shows how ranking works inside a category.

Category-based rounds supplement general and program-specific rounds. They do not increase the total number of invitations under the 2026-2028 Immigration Levels Plan. Your CRS score still decides your rank, but in a category round you are compared only with other candidates in that category.

Three kinds of invitation round

Round typeWho can be invitedHow ranking works
GeneralCandidates from all Express Entry programsHighest CRS scores in the whole pool
Program-specificCandidates eligible under one program, for example PNP or CECHighest CRS scores within that program
Category-basedCandidates in the pool who meet a category definitionHighest CRS scores within that category

Check the category-specific IRCC page before you rely on a category. The exact eligible occupations (NOC codes) and work-experience rules differ by category, and IRCC publishes them per category. This guide does not list NOC codes per category, because they change.

The 1-year experience rule

For all renewed categories, the minimum work experience rose from 6 months to 1 year of experience in an eligible occupation, gained in Canada or abroad over the previous 3 years. Six months of experience is no longer enough for a renewed category. If you qualified under the old 6-month rule, check whether you now meet the 1-year rule before counting on a category round.

For the new 2026 categories, check the category-specific IRCC pages for the exact work-experience requirements. This guide does not state them, because they differ by category.

Who can be invited in a category round

There is no separate category application. To be invited in a category round you must:

  • Already be in the Express Entry pool, which means you qualify under the Federal Skilled Worker Program, the Canadian Experience Class, or the Federal Skilled Trades Program.
  • Meet the definition of the category, including its occupation and work-experience rules.
  • Rank high enough by CRS score within that category when a round is held.

Keep your profile accurate and up to date. Your occupation, work history and language results are what make your category eligibility visible to IRCC.

Why cut-offs differ in category rounds

In a general round you compete with the whole pool. In a category round you compete only with candidates eligible for that category, a much smaller group. The lowest invited score in a category round depends on who is in that smaller group and how many invitations the round issues. That is why a category cut-off can be lower than a general round cut-off, and why it can also be higher in a popular category. There is no fixed category cut-off, and this guide publishes no cut-off numbers. Check the latest ministerial instructions for the most recent rounds.

Your CRS score still matters in every round type. A higher score ranks you higher whether the round is general, program-specific, or category-based. Check yours with the CRS Calculator.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a category-based draw?

A category-based draw is an Express Entry round in which IRCC invites candidates who are already in the pool and eligible for a specific category, such as a healthcare occupation or French-language proficiency. Candidates are ranked by CRS score within that category. Category-based selection started in 2023.

Do I need to apply for a category separately?

No. There is no separate category application. If you are in the Express Entry pool and you meet a category definition, IRCC can invite you in a category round. Keep your profile accurate and up to date so your category eligibility is visible.

Which categories are new in 2026?

The new 2026 categories are medical doctors with Canadian work experience, researchers with Canadian work experience, senior managers with Canadian work experience, transport occupations, and skilled military recruits with a job offer from the Canadian Armed Forces.

How much work experience do I need for a renewed category?

For all renewed categories, the minimum rose from 6 months to 1 year of experience in an eligible occupation, gained in Canada or abroad over the previous 3 years. Check the category-specific IRCC page for the exact counting rules for your category.

Why are cut-offs lower in some category rounds?

In a category round, you are ranked only against other candidates eligible for that category, a smaller group than the whole pool. The lowest invited score depends on who is in that smaller group and how many invitations the round issues, so it can be lower or higher than a general round. There is no fixed category cut-off.
