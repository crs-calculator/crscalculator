About this site

About Contact Privacy Policy Terms Disclaimer

CRS Calculator is an independent estimate tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) or the Government of Canada. Scoring rules are based on the IRCC CRS criteria, Express Entry changes, high-wage occupations and language test pages, read in November 2026. Always confirm your score with the official IRCC CRS tool and a licensed immigration professional.

All calculations happen in your browser. This site does not collect or store your inputs.
