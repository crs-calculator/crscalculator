Federal Skilled Trades Program (FST): Requirements, Explained

Last updated: November 18, 2026. Sources: IRCC Express Entry eligibility comparison page and Federal Skilled Trades Program help centre page, read November 2026.

The Federal Skilled Trades Program (FST) is the Express Entry program for skilled trades workers. It asks for 2 years of trade experience in the last 5 years, plus either a valid job offer or a Canadian certificate of qualification, and lower language minimums than the other two programs. There is no education requirement. This guide lists the eligible trade groups, explains the hour counting, and compares FST with the Canadian Experience Class and the Federal Skilled Worker Program.

Like the other programs, FST only gets you into the pool. Your CRS score decides whether you receive an invitation, so check your ranking with the CRS Calculator once you know you qualify.

Who FST is for

FST is for workers qualified in a skilled trade who want to become permanent residents. Your trade must be in an eligible group, your experience must be recent, and you must plan to live outside Quebec. There is no points grid like the FSW 67-point grid. You either meet the requirements or you do not.

Eligible trade groups

Eligible trades are in Training, Education, Experience and Responsibilities (TEER) 2 or 3. The groups are:

Check your NOC code before you apply. A trade that sounds eligible may sit in an excluded sub-group. Confirm your occupation NOC code and its group against the IRCC list. Experience in an ineligible group does not count, however long you worked.

Experience and hour counting

You need 2 years of work experience in a skilled trade within the last 5 years. That means 3,120 hours. Full-time work is up to 30 hours a week for 24 months, and hours above 30 a week do not count. Two more rules decide whether your experience counts:

  • You must have been qualified to practise the trade where you gained the experience. Experience gained before you were qualified does not count.
  • Work while studying does not count.

Job offer or certificate of qualification

You must have one of the following:

OptionRequirement
Valid job offerFull-time employment for at least 1 year, from a Canadian employer
Certificate of qualificationIssued by a Canadian provincial, territorial or federal authority, in your skilled trade

One of the two is required. Having neither means you do not qualify for FST, however strong your experience is.

Language requirements

FST has the lowest language minimums of the three programs, and the minimums differ by ability:

AbilityMinimum CLB or NCLC
SpeakingCLB 5
ListeningCLB 5
ReadingCLB 4
WritingCLB 4

Language test results must be less than 2 years old. Use the CLB Calculator to convert your test scores to CLB or NCLC levels per ability. Higher scores still earn more CRS points, so treat these minimums as a floor.

Proof of funds and Quebec

Proof of funds is needed under FST, unless you are authorized to work in Canada and have a valid job offer. See proof of funds for the amounts, the family-size counting, and the bank letter IRCC accepts. You must also plan to live outside Quebec, as Quebec selects its own skilled workers.

Common mistakes

  • Wrong trade group. Experience in an excluded sub-group or a non-trade occupation does not count.
  • Counting hours above 30 a week. Extra hours do not shorten the 24 months.
  • Counting study-time work. Work gained while studying full-time is excluded.
  • Neither offer nor certificate. One of the two is required. Start the certificate process early, as it takes time.
  • Forgetting proof of funds. FST needs proof of funds unless you are authorized to work in Canada with a valid job offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much trade experience do I need for the Federal Skilled Trades Program?

You need 2 years of work experience in a skilled trade within the last 5 years, equal to 3,120 hours. Full-time means up to 30 hours a week for 24 months, and hours above 30 a week do not count. The experience only counts if you were qualified to practise the trade where you gained it.

Do I need a job offer for the Federal Skilled Trades Program?

You need either a valid job offer of full-time employment for at least 1 year, or a certificate of qualification in your trade issued by a Canadian provincial, territorial or federal authority. One of the two is required.

What language level do I need for FST?

You need CLB 5 in speaking and listening, and CLB 4 in reading and writing. Language test results must be less than 2 years old.

Is education required for the Federal Skilled Trades Program?

No. There is no education requirement for the Federal Skilled Trades Program. Education can still earn CRS points, and foreign education needs an Educational Credential Assessment to claim those points.

Do I need proof of funds for FST?

Yes, unless you are authorized to work in Canada and have a valid job offer. Proof of funds is needed for the Federal Skilled Worker Program and the Federal Skilled Trades Program, but not for the Canadian Experience Class.
