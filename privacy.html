Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What we collect

Nothing from the calculator. The CRS Calculator runs entirely in your browser. The age, education, language, work and family details you enter are processed on your own device and are never sent to any server, stored, or shared with anyone.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use cookies, accounts, or analytics that identify you. Standard server logs kept by the hosting provider may record anonymous technical data such as the pages requested, the time of the request, and general device and browser information, as is normal for any website.

Contact emails

If you email us, we will use your message only to respond to you. We do not sell, rent, or share email addresses with third parties.

Advertising

This site currently shows no advertising. If advertising is added in the future, this policy will be updated before any advertising code goes live, and it will describe the cookies and data practices of the advertising provider.

External links

Links to IRCC and other official sources leave this site. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new update date. Questions can be sent through the contact page.

About this site

About Contact Privacy Policy Terms Disclaimer

CRS Calculator is an independent estimate tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) or the Government of Canada. Scoring rules are based on the IRCC CRS criteria, Express Entry changes, high-wage occupations and language test pages, read in November 2026. Always confirm your score with the official IRCC CRS tool and a licensed immigration professional.

All calculations happen in your browser. This site does not collect or store your inputs.
