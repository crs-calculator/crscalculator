Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

What we collect

Nothing from the calculator. The CRS Calculator runs entirely in your browser. The age, education, language, work and family details you enter are processed on your own device and are never sent to any server, stored, or shared with anyone.

Cookies and tracking

This site does not use accounts, and the calculator does not store or send your inputs anywhere. The site does not run its own tracking, but advertising partners described below may use cookies to show ads, and the hosting provider may collect anonymous performance data to measure page speed. Standard server logs kept by the hosting provider may record anonymous technical data such as the pages requested, the time of the request, and general device and browser information, as is normal for any website.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors use it. Google Analytics collects anonymous usage data such as the pages visited, the time spent on them, general device and browser information, and approximate location. It does not collect your calculator inputs, your name, or your email address. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Contact emails

If you email us, we will use your message only to respond to you. We do not sell, rent, or share email addresses with third parties.

Advertising

Advertising on this site is served by Google AdSense and other third party vendors. These vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info/choices. You can also opt out of a third party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting that vendor's opt out page.

External links

Links to IRCC and other official sources leave this site. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new update date. Questions can be sent through the contact page.
